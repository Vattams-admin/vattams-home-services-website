# VATTAMS Home Services — Marketing, Promotion & SEO Growth Plan

Last reviewed: 25 October 2026

## Goal

Grow qualified customer bookings and technician applications without advertising service availability, warranties, reviews, discounts or response times that cannot be verified.

Brand scope: VATTAMS Home Services only — AC, washing machine and refrigerator service. Do not mix in VATTAMS Academia.

## Positioning

**Core message:** Appliance service with clearer job selection, visible base pricing and appointment options checked for the customer's address.

Supporting proof points already reflected in the product:
- Exact AC job selection: General Service, Installation, Gas Filling, Pump Water Service and Coil Service.
- Washing machine and refrigerator service requests.
- Listed base prices and applicable GST/platform fees shown in the checkout breakdown.
- Appointment availability depends on address, job type and technician schedule.
- Parts/additional work should be quoted separately and approved before proceeding.
- Booking status and OTP checkpoints are supported in the workflow.

Avoid unverified claims such as “30-day warranty on every repair”, “free inspection”, “same-day guaranteed”, “background-verified/certified technicians”, fabricated customer testimonials, “lowest price”, or guaranteed arrival times.

## Current geographic strategy

Production technician records last checked on 11 October 2026 showed active technician records in Chennai (2) and Coimbatore (3). Use these as the first customer-acquisition markets, while checking actual booking availability by address.

- **Customer ads:** Start with Chennai and Coimbatore only. Exclude other cities from paid booking campaigns until technician supply and successful bookings are verified there.
- **Technician recruitment:** Prioritise cities where supply is missing or thin (including Madurai and other expansion targets). Do not imply that customer appointments are already available there.
- **Google Business Profile:** Use only a genuine, eligible business/service-area profile. Never create fake office addresses or duplicate city profiles. Keep phone, hours, service area and website consistent.

## 30-day launch plan

### Days 1–7 — Foundation
- Verify Google Search Console ownership and submit https://vattams.net/sitemap.xml.
- Verify GA4 events in DebugView/Realtime; check that booking success is counted once and no names, phone numbers or addresses are sent.
- Verify Google Business Profile eligibility and consistency. Add real service photos, hours and contact information.
- Test /services, /areas/chennai, /areas/coimbatore on mobile and desktop, including price loading, booking flow and contact links.
- Ask real customers for honest reviews after completed jobs; do not buy reviews, invent names or offer rewards for positive ratings.

### Days 8–14 — Organic short-form content
Publish 3–4 useful videos using real footage and permission:
1. “AC cooling low? 3 things to check before booking.”
2. “Washing machine not draining? What details to share when requesting service.”
3. “Fridge not cooling? Describe the symptoms clearly before a technician visit.”
4. “How VATTAMS booking works: choose job type, review price breakdown, check appointment options.”

Use Tamil voiceover/subtitles plus concise English captions. End with one clear CTA: **Check service options at vattams.net**. Only show real jobs and actual price screens.

### Days 15–21 — Local customer acquisition
- Test separate Google Search campaigns for AC, washing machine and refrigerator intent in Chennai and Coimbatore.
- Start with exact/phrase match; send each ad to the most relevant service page.
- Use negative keywords to separate technician recruitment from customer booking campaigns.
- Test a modest daily budget only after conversion tracking is confirmed. Pause keywords that spend without qualified booking requests.
- Use tracked UTM links for Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and WhatsApp campaigns.

### Days 22–30 — Improve conversion and supply
- Compare booking starts, successful requests, contact clicks and cost per qualified booking by city/service.
- Improve the landing page with the highest drop-off; do not change several variables at once.
- Run technician recruitment creatives for cities without active supply. Explain the fixed job-specific platform call-rate fee before technicians join.
- Expand customer ads to a new city only after active technician supply, successful test bookings and operational coverage are confirmed.

## Conversion and campaign improvements — 25 October 2026

### Customer pricing presentation
- Home and Services pages read job-specific base prices from the live `customer_service_prices` catalogue.
- Price cards now show a transparent estimated checkout total using the matching active `service_prices` GST rate and customer platform fee.
- Booking checkout remains the final source of truth for applicable charges and discounts.
- Keep technician-paid call-rate fees out of customer ads, customer price labels and customer checkout totals.
- Keep the distinction visible: base price, GST, customer platform fee, estimated total; parts or extra work require a separate quote and customer approval.

### Updated campaign creative framework
Use a repeatable 15–25 second vertical video structure for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels:
1. **0–3 sec — relatable problem:** “AC cooling kammiya?”, “Washing machine drain aagala?”, or “Fridge cool aagala?”
2. **3–8 sec — useful detail:** show one safe, practical observation or the information customers should share when booking. Do not instruct viewers to perform unsafe electrical or refrigerant work.
3. **8–15 sec — product proof:** screen-record the real service selector and current price breakdown; blur all personal/customer information.
4. **15–20 sec — transparent expectation:** explain that appointment options depend on address and technician schedule, and extra parts/work need approval.
5. **Final CTA — one action:** “Check service options at vattams.net/services” or “Choose your appointment options at vattams.net/booking”.

### Publishing cadence and channel roles
- **3 short videos per week:** one AC topic, one washing-machine topic, one refrigerator topic.
- **1 price-transparency post per week:** show the current catalogue and checkout breakdown; verify prices from production on publishing day.
- **1 technician recruitment post per week:** focus on actual service categories, onboarding steps, service-area demand and the disclosed job-specific platform call-rate terms.
- **WhatsApp:** use a short message with one tracked link; avoid unsolicited bulk messaging and provide an opt-out for promotional lists.
- **Google Search:** separate customer booking campaigns from technician recruitment campaigns. Keep recruitment keywords negative in customer campaigns and customer-service keywords negative in recruitment campaigns.
- **Local SEO:** focus customer-acquisition content on Chennai and Coimbatore until coverage and successful booking capacity are verified elsewhere. Other-city recruitment must not imply customer availability.

### Measurement and iteration
Use separate UTMs by channel and creative, for example `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=appliance_service&utm_content=ac_general_service_01`. Never put a customer's name, phone number, address or booking identifier in a URL.

Every two weeks, compare:
- Service page visits → booking starts → successful booking requests.
- Conversion rate by appliance, city and source.
- Calls/WhatsApp clicks and how many lead to a serviceable booking.
- Paid cost per qualified booking, cancellations and rejected assignments.
- Technician applications, approvals, online coverage and completed jobs.

Do not scale ad spend based on reach alone. Scale only when the booking flow, city coverage, technician capacity and cost per qualified booking are acceptable.

## Measurement

The website now emits these privacy-conscious GA4 events:
- page_view
- view_service_prices
- booking_start
- booking_request_attempt
- generate_lead after a booking request succeeds
- contact_click with phone/WhatsApp method only
- technician_join_page_view

Review weekly:
- Search Console impressions, clicks, queries and indexed URLs
- Qualified booking requests by city and service
- Booking-start → successful-request conversion
- Phone/WhatsApp clicks and response-to-booking rate
- Ad spend and cost per qualified booking
- Technician applications, approvals, online availability and completed jobs
- Cancellations, rejected assignments and customer complaints

Do not optimise for views alone. Optimise for completed, serviceable bookings and sustainable technician supply.
